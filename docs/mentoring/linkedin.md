“I should learn AI” is a difficult place to start. In my mentoring work, we start instead with something you actually care about: a project you keep postponing, a way of working you can picture but haven't figured out how to build, or a recurring task that takes more energy than it deserves.

My background spans technology, product work, psychology, coaching, and creative practice. During our sessions, we open your actual material and do a piece of the work together so continuing on your own becomes easier. Between meetings, you test things in daily life, and we look honestly at what worked, what felt awkward, and what got in the way.

Our goal is practical change in how you work day to day, built through small repeated actions that grow your confidence to continue independently.
